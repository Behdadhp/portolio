German tax notes

Sparer-Pauschbetrag (Freibetrag). Annual tax-free allowance on capital gains: €1,000 single, €2,000 joint. Applied to combined Net of stocks and ETFs. Remainder taxed at 25% Kapitalertragsteuer + 5.5% Soli = 26.375%. Church tax not included.

ETF Teilfreistellung. This estimate does NOT apply the partial-exemption (typically 30% for equity ETFs / 15% for mixed). Your real tax is usually lower than what's shown here.

Crypto (§23 EStG). Lots held longer than 365 days are 100% tax-free regardless of size. Lots sold inside 365 days fall under the €1,000 Freigrenze: at or under the limit it's tax-free; once you cross it, the ENTIRE short-term net is taxable at your personal income-tax rate.

Loss-offset rules. Stock losses offset stock gains; ETF losses offset ETF gains; crypto short-term losses offset crypto short-term gains. Cross-class offsetting is generally not allowed.

Estimate only. Always cross-check with your Steuerberater or your broker's annual Steuerbescheinigung before filing.
