Notes & methodology

Cost basis. Stocks & ETFs use weighted-average cost basis (buys add price × amount + fee; sells reduce units leaving the average per-unit cost unchanged). Crypto uses FIFO — each sell is matched against the oldest unsold buy lots.

Realized vs Unrealized P&L. Realized is locked in by past sells (sell price − weighted-avg or FIFO lot price, minus the sell fee). Unrealized is current market value minus cost basis of units still held; it becomes realized the moment those units are sold.

Current price source. Live prices are pulled from Finnhub (stocks and ETFs) and CoinGecko / Binance (crypto). Assets with no cached live price show "—" and are excluded from current value.

Real P&L vs invested. Compares mark-to-market portfolio worth to your cumulative cash deposits minus withdrawals. Includes both realized and unrealized P&L on a single line.
